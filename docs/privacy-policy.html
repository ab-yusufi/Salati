Privacy Policy for SalahPulse

Effective date: August 1, 2026

SalahPulse is an offline Salah and habit tracker. This Privacy Policy explains what information the app handles, how it is stored, and the choices available to users.

1. Developer and contact

SalahPulse is provided by AB Coder. Questions about this Privacy Policy may be sent to abyusufi101@gmail.com.

2. Information handled by the app

SalahPulse allows users to create and manage information such as:

  • Prayer records and prayer-status selections;
  • Habit names, habit records, and completion statuses;
  • User-configured reminder times;
  • Notification, appearance, and theme preferences; and
  • Calendar history and statistics generated from locally stored records.

Prayer and habit records may reflect personal religious practices or routines. SalahPulse processes this information locally on the user's device.

3. How information is used

Information entered into SalahPulse is used only to provide the app's features, including daily tracking, history, insights, reminders, and notification actions.

4. Local storage and data transmission

SalahPulse does not require an account. The current version does not transmit prayer records, habit records, reminder settings, or other tracking information to the developer or to a SalahPulse-operated server.

SalahPulse does not include advertising or analytics services in the current release.

5. Permissions

SalahPulse may use the following Android capabilities:

  • Notifications: Used to display prayer and habit reminders configured by the user. Users may deny or disable notifications through Android settings.
  • Start after device restart: Used to restore configured reminders after the device restarts or relevant system time settings change.

SalahPulse does not request location permission and does not calculate prayer times from the user's location.

6. Android backup and device transfer

Depending on the user's device, Android version, Google account, and system backup settings, Android may back up or transfer some app data. This process is controlled by the Android operating system and the user's device settings, not by a SalahPulse-operated server.

7. Data sharing

The current version of SalahPulse does not sell, rent, or share users' tracking information with advertisers, data brokers, or other companies.

8. Data retention and deletion

Locally stored information remains on the device until it is changed or deleted by the user, the app's storage is cleared, or the app is uninstalled.

Users may clear supported prayer or habit records within the app and may delete habits through the habit-management features. Users may also remove locally stored app data through Android's app-storage settings or by uninstalling SalahPulse.

SalahPulse does not maintain a server-side user account or a developer-controlled copy of tracking records. Data retained in an Android backup is governed by the device and backup provider's settings and retention practices.

9. Security

SalahPulse stores its records in the app's local private storage. Users should protect access to their device using appropriate Android security features. No method of electronic storage can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

10. Children's privacy

SalahPulse does not knowingly collect personal information from children or any other users through a developer-operated server. The app does not provide accounts, social features, advertising, or public sharing.

11. Changes to this policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when SalahPulse's features or data practices change. The revised policy will show an updated effective date. Material changes will be reflected in the app or its store listing where appropriate.

12. Contact

For privacy-related questions, contact: abyusufi101@gmail.com.
